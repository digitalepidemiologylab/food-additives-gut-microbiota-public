# Analysis

Each subfolder contains all analyses used to generate the figures, statistics, and results reported in the manuscript.
`figures/` and `outputs/` folders contain the main figures and Supplementary files tables generated by the analyses.

---

## Additive mixtures

### `mixtures_analysis/`

* `mixtures_generation.ipynb`
  Identifies real-world additive co-consumption patterns using non-negative matrix factorization (NMF). Outputs mixture loadings and participant-level mixture scores.

* `mixtures_analysis.ipynb`
  Descriptive analyses of additive mixtures, including prevalence, dietary and demographic correlations, and mixture dominance patterns.

* `additive_and_mixture_effects.ipynb`
  Association analyses between additive classes, additive mixtures, and gut microbiota alpha diversity.

---

## Groupwise comparisons

### `group_comparisons/`

* `additive_intake_extremes_analysis.ipynb`
  Extreme-quartile analyses comparing additive intake across dietary, demographic, and lifestyle variables.

* `with_additives.ipynb`
  Non-parametric comparisons of gut microbial diversity across low vs high additive intake groups.

* `with_mixtures.ipynb`
  Non-parametric comparisons of gut microbial diversity across additive mixture exposure groups.

* `high_vegfruits_and_addives.ipynb`
  Stratified analyses among high vegetable or fruit consumers, comparing low vs high additive exposure (attenuation analyses).

* `nova_analysis.ipynb`
  Correlations between NOVA food-processing groups and additive exposure, and additive models adjusted for NOVA intake.

* `barcoded_sugar_analysis.ipynb`
  Sugar intake from barcoded foods and its role in the sweetener association (nested, residualized, and mediation models).

---

## Food group associations

### `food_group_associations/`

* `food_group_association_analysis.ipynb`
  Associations between food group consumption, additive exposure, and gut microbial diversity.

---

## Beta diversity and ordination

### `pcoa_analysis/`

* `pcoa_plot_total_additives.R`
  PCoA visualization of gut microbiota beta diversity with total additive intake gradients.

* `pcoa_envfit.R`
  Environmental fitting of additives, mixtures, food groups, and dietary variables onto ordination space.

* `permanova_total_additive.R`
  PERMANOVA and dispersion testing for additive effects on beta diversity.

---

## Differential abundance and microbial log-ratios

### `birdman_analysis/`

* `DA_analysis.ipynb`
  Differential abundance analysis using BIRDMAn to identify taxa associated with additives, mixtures, and dietary variables.

* `extract_DA_taxa_and_LR.ipynb`
  Construction of microbial log-ratio metrics from BIRDMAn results and correlation with gut microbial diversity.

---

## Functional pathway associations

### `picrust_associations/`

* `maaslin_analysis.ipynb`
  Multivariable association testing between PICRUSt2-predicted pathways and additive exposures using MaAsLin2.

* `picrust_ols_associations.ipynb`
  Complementary OLS-based pathway association analyses and validation.

* `picrust_maaslin.R`
  R implementation of MaAsLin2 pathway association models.

---

## Machine learning

### `machine_learning/`

* `run_model.py`
  XGBoost training script for classification and regression of dietary and additive exposures from microbiome data.

* `plotting_ml_results.ipynb`
  Visualization of model performance and comparative results.
